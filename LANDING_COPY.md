## FAQ

**Q: Is BurnLens really free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no signup, no catch. We show you your audit
instantly. We only ask for your email if you want to save the report.

**Q: How does BurnLens make money?**
A: BurnLens is built by Credex, a marketplace for discounted AI
infrastructure credits. If your audit shows significant savings,
we may suggest Credex as a way to save even more. That's it.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Your tool and spend information is used only to generate your audit.
We do not sell it, share it, or use it to train models.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price in our audit engine is verified weekly against official
vendor pricing pages. Sources are documented in our public GitHub repo.

**Q: What if I disagree with a recommendation?**
A: Fair enough. Our recommendations are based on general usage patterns.
Your workflow may justify a premium plan. The audit is a starting point
for a conversation, not a directive.
